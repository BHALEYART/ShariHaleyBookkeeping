How this estimate works

The calculator multiplies the hours you spend on your books each month by 12, then by what an hour of your time is worth. If your CPA bills you to clean up your books before preparing your return, that cost is added too.

  • It’s an estimate, not a quote. It shows what your current approach costs, not what you’d save. That depends on your business and on what bookkeeping would cost you.
  • Your time has a price even if you don’t pay yourself by the hour. Hours on bookkeeping are hours not spent serving customers, selling, or resting.
  • Some costs aren’t counted, like late fees, missed deductions, or a loan delayed because your financial statements weren’t ready.
