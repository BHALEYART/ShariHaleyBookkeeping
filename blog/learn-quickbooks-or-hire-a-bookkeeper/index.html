Should you learn QuickBooks yourself or hire a bookkeeper?

Weighing QuickBooks training against hiring a bookkeeper? Compare time, cost, and risk, and see when QuickBooks certification makes sense for your staff.

By Shari Haley, QuickBooks Platinum Certified ProAdvisor · November 23, 2026 · 2 min read

Short answer: learn QuickBooks yourself (or train a staff member) if you have the time, fairly simple finances, and want hands-on control. Hire a bookkeeper if you’re short on time, behind, or need payroll and reconciliations done right every month. Many businesses do both: a professional keeps the books, and the owner learns enough QuickBooks to read the reports and handle daily tasks.

When learning QuickBooks makes sense

  • You have a manageable number of transactions each month
  • You or someone on staff has time set aside for bookkeeping every week
  • You want to understand your numbers in detail, not just receive reports
  • You’re hiring an office manager or admin who will own day-to-day entries

The catch is learning it correctly. Self-taught QuickBooks often works fine until reconciliation, payroll, or tax time reveals months of small errors. A few hours of training at the start can prevent a costly clean-up later.

When hiring a bookkeeper makes sense

  • Your books are already behind, or you don’t trust them
  • You run payroll or have several bank and credit card accounts
  • Your time is worth more on revenue-generating work
  • You want reports your CPA can use without rework

See 7 signs it’s time for a bookkeeper for a closer look.

The middle path: a bookkeeper plus training

Some owners want a professional handling reconciliations, payroll, and month-end, but also want their team entering invoices and bills correctly. Training your staff on exactly those tasks keeps things moving day to day while the bookkeeper keeps everything accurate.

What QuickBooks training covers

  • Company setup and a chart of accounts that fits your business
  • Bank feeds, categorizing transactions, and reconciliations
  • Invoicing, bills, and tracking who owes what
  • Running payroll in QuickBooks
  • Reading your profit and loss statement and balance sheet
  • QuickBooks Online and QuickBooks Desktop

Should your staff get QuickBooks certified?

Intuit offers certification exams, such as QuickBooks Online Certified User, that show someone can use the software proficiently. Certification can be worthwhile for an office manager or bookkeeper on your team, and it’s a credential they carry with them. Shari helps people prepare for these exams as part of training.

Quick answers

Is QuickBooks hard to learn?

The basics are approachable, but small setup mistakes (like the wrong chart of accounts or skipped reconciliations) can quietly throw off your numbers for months. Training from someone who uses QuickBooks every day helps you avoid those traps.

What is QuickBooks certification?

Intuit, the maker of QuickBooks, offers certification exams that show someone can use the software proficiently, such as QuickBooks Online Certified User. The exams are scheduled through Intuit’s testing partner. Shari can help your staff prepare.

Do you train on QuickBooks Desktop or QuickBooks Online?

Both. Training is tailored to the version your business uses and the tasks your team actually does.
