FAQ

Common bookkeeping questions

What’s the difference between a bookkeeper and an accountant?

Bookkeepers focus on the transactional side — recording, categorizing, and reconciling every transaction. Accountants focus on analysis, reporting, and decision-making built on those records. Clean books make your accountant’s job faster and more accurate.

My books are months behind. Can you help?

Yes. Our catch-up service works through your backlog of bank and credit card transactions so your records are current again — and you get your time back.

Another bookkeeper did my books and I’m not sure they’re right. What should I do?

A clean-up reviews your books for errors and omissions — including work done by less experienced bookkeepers — corrects them, and sets up a streamlined system to keep everything current going forward.

Do you work with QuickBooks?

Yes. Shari is a QuickBooks ProAdvisor with Gold Tier and Payroll certifications. If you’re on spreadsheets, another program, or nothing at all yet, mention it in your consultation request and we’ll talk through the best setup for you.

Do you handle payroll?

Yes. Payroll support covers setup and processing, wages, benefits and deductions, payroll tax compliance, and reporting and filings.
