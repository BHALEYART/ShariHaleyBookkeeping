Short answer: catch-up bookkeeping works through your backlog of bank and credit card transactions, months or even years of them, and brings your books current and reconciled. It’s the fastest way to get from “I don’t know where we stand” to accurate numbers you and your CPA can rely on.

Falling behind is one of the most common things we see. It usually isn’t carelessness; it’s a busy season, a staff change, or a business that grew faster than its systems. The good news: it’s fixable, and you don’t have to do it alone.

Signs you need catch-up bookkeeping

  • Your bank feed has hundreds of uncategorized transactions
  • Accounts haven’t been reconciled in months
  • Your CPA has asked for reports you can’t produce
  • A tax deadline, loan application, or IRS or state notice is coming
  • You’ve avoided opening QuickBooks because you know what’s waiting

How catch-up bookkeeping works

  1. Review. We look at your current books (QuickBooks, spreadsheets, or nothing yet) and figure out where they stop.
  2. Gather. You provide statements and documents for the missing period (see the list below).
  3. Record and categorize. Every transaction is entered and categorized so it lands in the right place on your tax return.
  4. Reconcile. Bank and credit card accounts are reconciled month by month, so the books match the statements.
  5. Report. You get current financial reports and a clear list of anything that needs your input.
  6. Stay current. We set you up with a simple system, or ongoing bookkeeping, so it doesn’t happen again.

What to gather before you start

  • Bank and credit card statements for every month that’s behind
  • Loan statements and any merchant or payment-processor reports
  • Payroll reports, if you have employees
  • Sales records, invoices, and bills
  • Receipts you have (don’t worry if some are missing)
  • Your last filed tax return, if available

Caught up but still not sure the numbers are right? Books done by a previous bookkeeper, or by you in a rush, can be current but inaccurate. That’s what a bookkeeping clean-up is for.

After you’re caught up

The real win is staying current. Most clients move to monthly or quarterly bookkeeping so their books are always ready for tax time and decisions. Prefer to handle it yourself? QuickBooks training can set you up with a routine that sticks.
